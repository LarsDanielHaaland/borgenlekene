# Borgenlekene

A portal for organizing events where friends take part in activities and compare results.

## Language

**Nickname claim**:
A link between a signed-in user account and a participant nickname within one event. A nickname claim is distinct from accepting an invitation, and a user may claim at most one nickname per event.

**Manual ranking**:
An explicitly chosen order of participants in an Activity or tournament round, rather than a ranking calculated from match or measured results.
_Avoid_: Manual order
