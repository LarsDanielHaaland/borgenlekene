# Borgenlekene

A portal for organizing events where friends take part in activities and compare results.

## Language

**Nickname claim**:
A link between a signed-in user account and a participant nickname within one event. A nickname claim is distinct from accepting an invitation, and a user may claim at most one nickname per event.

**Manual ranking**:
An explicitly chosen order of participants in an Activity or tournament round, rather than a ranking calculated from match or measured results.
_Avoid_: Manual order

**Ranking outcome**:
The complete participant order and derived standings produced after a result, manual ranking, or randomized ranking is accepted for an event activity.
_Avoid_: Partial ranking, score update

**Result recording**:
The act of accepting a measured or match result for an event activity and incorporating it into the activity’s ranking outcome.
_Avoid_: Score update

**Standings recalculation**:
The derivation of activity standings and event totals from the accepted results and rankings for an event.
_Avoid_: Manual score adjustment
